User Data Deletion

Last updated: October 19, 2026

The Micke app does not store personal data of Facebook users on any server. If you have interacted with our Pages and want any related data removed, you can:

Option 1 – Remove the App from Facebook

  1. Go to Facebook Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Open Apps and websites (Business integrations).
  3. Find Micke and click Remove.

Option 2 – Email us

Send an email to jenjp789@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and your Facebook name or the link to your comment. We will delete any related data within 30 days and confirm by email.
