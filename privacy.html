Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Micke app ("the App", "we") handles information when it is used with Facebook.

1. Who uses the App

The App is operated only by the owner of the connected Facebook Pages. It is not offered to the public and does not let other people log in.

2. Information we access

  • A Page access token for the Pages managed by the owner, used to publish and schedule posts, photos, videos and Reels.
  • Basic Page information (Page ID, Page name) needed to publish content.
  • Public comments on the owner's own Page posts, only when needed to read or reply to them.

We do not collect passwords, payment information, private messages, contact lists, or any data about people's Facebook profiles beyond what Facebook shows publicly with a comment.

3. How we use information

Information is used only to publish learning content and manage the owner's Pages. We do not sell, rent, or share data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens are kept on the owner's own computer and are never published. Simple logs (such as post IDs and times) are kept locally to avoid posting the same content twice.

5. Data retention

Data is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above and can be deleted at any time. See User Data Deletion.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always available on this page.
